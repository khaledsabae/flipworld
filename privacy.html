FLIPWORLD — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

FLIPWORLD ("the game") is published by Khaled Sabae. Contact: khalidhamdy50@gmail.com.

What the game itself collects

The game does not have accounts, chat or purchases, and it does not send your gameplay data to any server operated by the developer.

Your progress (finished levels, stars, gems, selected ball, sound and control settings, daily streak) is saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising in the Android app

The Android app shows ads using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from the IP address, and ad interaction and diagnostic data.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), you are asked for your consent before any ad is requested, and you can refuse. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Web version

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses in order to deliver the site (see GitHub's privacy statement). The game adds no analytics or tracking of its own and shows no ads on this site.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices and requests

Because the developer does not hold personal data about players, there is nothing to export or delete on the developer's side. For questions, write to the contact address above.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
